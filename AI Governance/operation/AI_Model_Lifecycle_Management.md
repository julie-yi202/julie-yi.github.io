# AI Model Lifecycle Management

1. Purpose

This document outlines the comprehensive lifecycle management process for AI models within the organization. It ensures models are developed, deployed, monitored, and retired in a controlled, compliant, and ethical manner aligned with GDPR, EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and NIST AI RMF standards.

2. Scope

All AI models developed or used internally

AI models integrated via APIs or third-party services

All departments involved in AI model lifecycle activities

All data processed by AI models

3. Model Lifecycle Phases

3.1 Planning & Requirements

Define business objectives

Identify data requirements

Assess ethical, legal, and compliance considerations

3.2 Data Collection & Preparation

Collect relevant datasets

Perform data cleaning and preprocessing

Ensure data privacy and security compliance

3.3 Model Development

Select appropriate algorithms and architectures

Train models using prepared data

Validate model performance and fairness

3.4 Testing & Validation

Conduct rigorous testing for accuracy, bias, and robustness

Perform security and privacy assessments

Obtain stakeholder approvals

3.5 Deployment

Deploy models to production environments

Implement monitoring and alerting mechanisms

Establish rollback and update procedures

3.6 Monitoring & Maintenance

Continuously monitor model performance and behavior

Detect model drift and data quality issues

Apply updates, retraining, or decommissioning as needed

3.7 Retirement

Plan model decommissioning

Archive model artifacts and documentation

Ensure data retention and deletion policies are followed

4. Roles & Responsibilities

AI Governance Lead

Oversees lifecycle management

Ensures compliance and ethical standards

Data Scientist / ML Engineer

Develops and validates models

Monitors model performance

Data Engineer

Manages data pipelines and quality

Security Lead

Ensures security controls for models and data

Privacy Officer / DPO

Oversees data privacy compliance

Product Owner

Aligns model outcomes with business goals

5. Risk Management

Identify risks at each lifecycle phase

Implement mitigation strategies

Maintain risk register and update regularly

6. Documentation & Reporting

Maintain detailed records of model design, training, testing, deployment, and monitoring

Produce audit reports and compliance evidence

7. Continuous Improvement

Review lifecycle processes periodically

Incorporate lessons learned and emerging best practices

Update policies and procedures accordingly

8. Compliance & Ethics

Adhere to GDPR, EU AI Act, and other relevant regulations

Ensure transparency, fairness, and accountability

Conduct ethical impact assessments

9. Tools & Infrastructure

Use approved platforms and tools for model development and deployment

Implement secure environments for data and model handling

10. Training & Awareness

Provide ongoing training for stakeholders involved in AI model lifecycle

Promote awareness of risks, compliance, and ethical considerations

11. Review & Audit

Schedule regular audits of AI models and lifecycle processes

Address findings with corrective actions

12. Completion Criteria

Model lifecycle phase is considered complete when:

Objectives for the phase are met

Compliance and quality checks are passed

Documentation is finalized and approved
