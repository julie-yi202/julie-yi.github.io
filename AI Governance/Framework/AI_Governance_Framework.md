## **AI Governance Framework for OpenAI GPT Systems**

### **1. Purpose**
This framework establishes how the organization governs the use of OpenAI GPT models (GPT‑4o, GPT‑4, GPT‑3.5). It defines principles, roles, processes, and controls to ensure safe, ethical, secure, and compliant AI usage.

### **2. Scope**
Applies to:
- All OpenAI-powered applications  
- All employees interacting with GPT systems  
- All customer-facing AI features  
- All internal productivity tools using GPT  

### **3. Governance Principles**
- **Accountability** — Clear ownership of AI decisions and risks  
- **Transparency** — Users informed when AI is used  
- **Fairness** — Bias testing and mitigation  
- **Privacy** — GDPR-compliant processing  
- **Security** — Protection against adversarial threats  
- **Human Oversight** — No fully automated decisions  
- **Compliance** — EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF  

### **4. Governance Structure**
- **AI Governance Lead** — Owns framework  
- **Technical Owner** — Manages integration  
- **Risk Owner** — Oversees risk assessments  
- **Human Oversight Owner** — Ensures HITL/HOTL  
- **Legal & Compliance** — Ensures regulatory alignment  

### **5. Lifecycle Governance**
- **Intake & Approval** — Use case approval process  
- **Design** — Risk assessment, DPIA  
- **Development** — Secure coding, prompt safety  
- **Testing** — Bias, robustness, adversarial testing  
- **Deployment** — Transparency, oversight, monitoring  
- **Monitoring** — Drift, incidents, logs  
- **Retirement** — Documentation, data deletion  

### **6. Required Documentation**
- Model Card  
- Dataset Card (if fine-tuned)  
- DPIA  
- EU AI Act Checklist  
- Risk Register  
- Incident Response Plan  
- Monitoring Plan  

### **7. Prohibited Uses**
- Biometric identification  
- Emotion recognition  
- HR screening  
- Credit scoring  
- Predictive policing  
- Manipulation of vulnerable groups  

### **8. Compliance Alignment**
- GDPR  
- EU AI Act (GPAI classification)  
- ISO 42001  
- NIST AI RMF
