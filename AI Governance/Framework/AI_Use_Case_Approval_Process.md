
# **AI_Use_Case_Approval_Process_IMAGINARYai.md**

## **AI Use Case Approval Process for IMAGINARYai GPT Systems**

### **1. Purpose**
Defines how AI use cases are evaluated, approved, or rejected.

### **2. Intake Form Requirements**
- Description of use case  
- Intended users  
- Data involved  
- Expected outputs  
- Risks  
- Oversight needs  
- Compliance considerations  

### **3. Approval Workflow**
1. **Submission** — Product Owner  
2. **Initial Review** — AI Governance Lead  
3. **Risk Assessment** — Risk Owner  
4. **DPIA (if needed)** — Legal  
5. **Security Review** — Security Lead  
6. **Compliance Review** — Legal  
7. **Oversight Plan** — Human Oversight Owner  
8. **Final Approval** — Governance Lead + Legal  

### **4. Approval Criteria**
- Alignment with governance principles  
- Acceptable risk level  
- Adequate human oversight  
- Compliance with GDPR & EU AI Act  
- No prohibited uses  
- Technical feasibility  

### **5. Outcomes**
- **Approved**  
- **Approved with conditions**  
- **Requires redesign**  
- **Rejected**
