## **AI Accountability Model for IMAGINARYai GPT Systems**

### **1. Purpose**
Defines who is responsible for decisions, risks, oversight, and compliance when using IMAGINARYai GPT models.

### **2. Roles & Responsibilities**

#### **AI Governance Lead**
- Owns governance framework  
- Approves AI use cases  
- Oversees compliance  

#### **Technical Owner**
- Manages IMAGINARYai API integration  
- Implements safety filters  
- Ensures secure deployment  

#### **Risk Owner**
- Conducts AI risk assessments  
- Maintains risk register  
- Ensures mitigation measures  

#### **Human Oversight Owner**
- Ensures HITL/HOTL/HIC  
- Reviews sensitive outputs  
- Oversees escalation  

#### **Legal & Compliance**
- Reviews GDPR implications  
- Ensures EU AI Act compliance  
- Manages vendor agreements  

#### **Security Lead**
- Implements AI-specific security controls  
- Monitors for adversarial attacks  
- Oversees incident response  

### **3. Decision Rights**
- **Approve AI use cases:** Governance Lead + Legal  
- **Approve deployment:** Governance Lead + Security  
- **Override AI decisions:** Human Oversight Owner  
- **Suspend system:** Security Lead  

### **4. Accountability for Outcomes**
- **Model outputs:** Technical Owner + Human Oversight  
- **Bias & fairness:** Risk Owner  
- **Privacy compliance:** Legal  
- **Security posture:** Security Lead
